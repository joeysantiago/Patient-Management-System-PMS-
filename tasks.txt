PROJECT OVERVIEW
--------------------------------------------------------------------
Single-user, web-based Patient Management App for a general physician.
Core flows: Patient records -> Appointments -> Consultation
(Vitals -> Complaints -> Diagnosis -> Prescription) -> History -> Export.

SUCCESS CRITERIA (for validation before sign-off)
--------------------------------------------------------------------
- Consultation record completed in 2-3 minutes
- Patient search/history retrieval in 2-5 seconds
- >= 80% reduction in paper usage (qualitative, post-UAT feedback)
- Smooth prescription generation & printing
- Successful CSV/PDF export
- Minimal training needed for the doctor to use the system
